Option A

By phase

Reads like a production line. Time = x-axis. Easy to teach — canonical order is literally left→right. Trade-off: hides that Ace re-enters (shown as two nodes).
Option B

By authority level

human · orchestrator · advisors · executors — vertical tiers
Hierarchy at a glance. Clear who can override whom. Good for onboarding. Trade-off: flattens the debate loop — doesn't show Ace speaking twice.
Option C

By invocation frequency

always-on inner · conditional outer · concentric around Ace
Ring = how often it fires. Inner always, outer conditional. Nova's optional nature falls out naturally from position. Trade-off: the canonical order becomes a squiggly orbit.
Option D

By input/output role

critics feed ← Ace → builders output · mirrored butterfly
Visceral: left speaks, right builds. Ace is the hinge. Symmetry makes the Nova/Vera optional branches read as "outer mirror members". Trade-off: speaking order not encoded; needs numbering overlay.
Option E

Debate loop

canonical speaking order as a clock · time flows clockwise
Emphasizes the loop — Ace opens AND closes at 12. Nova hangs off the loop instead of sitting in it, which is honest. Trade-off: less clear who outranks whom.
